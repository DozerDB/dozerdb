DozerDB third-party notices
===========================

This file lists the third-party components that DozerDB adds to Neo4j
Community, in whichever delivery form carries them. Neo4j's own third-party
notices are in Neo4j's ThirdPartyLicenses.txt and are not repeated here.

Every component below is licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0. The
licence text is at https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0 and is included
in ThirdPartyLicenses.txt of the DozerDB distribution, inherited from Neo4j
Community, and in browser/LICENSES.txt inside the DozerDB plugin JAR.
